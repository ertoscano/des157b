Technical Requirements

In my capstone project I anticipate using the following resources:

  • Back4App
  • Easy Timer
  • Get Well Soon Bill (for reference)
  • gsap

When the user presses a button that says "Please!" they will be brought to a screen that resembles a traffic light with a timer that goes up. The user can decide when to leave the traffic stop by pressing a button with a walk icon. They are then directed to a form that prompts them the question "What were you thinking of?"This response is then posted publicly to the bottom of the home screen. Users can see their initials, how long they were bored for, and what they were thinking about in the time they were bored.

I believe since I have a good reference project to get started I will be able to pull this project off in the time that we have. I will need to look more closly to the "Get Well Soon Bill" project JavaScript to get a better idea.
