Eric Toscano's Capstone Proposal

What is your topic?

My topic is mental health. In the age of constant intakes of dopamine, we need to take seriously the effect that it has on our mental health. My lense I will be exploring is challenging mental models. The challenging mental model I will be presenting is being okay with boredom. A change of one's mind is the primary way to bring results. Therefore I want to bring in a mental model that will have my audience question how they view boredom/doing nothing.

What are some of the features you imagine your project/product would include?

I will like to include a feature in my project where after a time of boredom, a user can submit a comment of what they were thinking of while in the time of boredom. These thinking posts will be displayed on the main page for everyone to see. I will also like to include GSAP library into this project to have the text appear nicely.

Are there possible tangents, challenges, concerns, or vague ideas related to your ideas?

My original plan for the capstone project is to have the user flip between a point of view of a person doing nothing and then to a point of view of a person watching their phone. Now I'm thinking of expanding on my Library Liberty project idea. I will have a timer and a boring background. The user can then stop the timer and write down what they were thinking about during that time.

Who is your target audience?

My target audience are students and adults who find themselves always looking for something to do and don't stop to sit still and think. When you scroll on social media your consuming. When you sit still with boredom you think. In the busyness of life can we as a society be still?
